<analysis>**original_problem_statement**:
Build an attendance management app where employees can apply for leaves (Out of Office - OOO) or Work From Home (WFH), and the requests are deducted from their leave balance.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS**:
1.  **Leave Balance Calculation**: Balances are based on the employee's joining month.
    *   Earned Leave (EL): 1.5 per month (18 per year).
    *   Casual/Sick Leave (CL/SL): 0.5 per month (6 per year).
    *   Work From Home (WFH): 1 per month (12 per year).
2.  **Leave Deduction Logic**: OOO requests first deduct from the CL/SL balance, and then from the EL balance.
3.  **EL Carry Forward**: Unused ELs can be carried forward, capped at 50% of the year's eligible ELs (e.g., if eligible for 18 ELs, 9 can be carried forward).
4.  **Roles & Permissions**:
    *   **HR**: Can add employees (including joining date, email, carry-forward ELs), add national holidays, add/remove leaves on behalf of employees.
    *   **Employee**: Can apply for leaves, view their own leave balance/history, and view the public leave calendar.
5.  **Calendar**: A shared calendar must be visible to all employees, showing who is on leave (OOO or WFH) on any given day.
6.  **Leave Application Flow**:
    *   Employees apply using their work email ID.
    *   No approval is needed for leave applications.
    *   The leave calendar and balances are updated automatically.
7.  **Notifications**: HR should be notified via email when an employee applies for leave.
8.  **Leave Year**: The leave cycle is from January to December.
9.  **Design**: Simple and clean, incorporating a tortoise logo.

**User's preferred language**: English
